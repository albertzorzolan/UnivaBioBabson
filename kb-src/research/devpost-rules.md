October 7th, 2026 12 AM CST --> Hackathon End

#### Eligibility

All students aged 13+ are eligible to participate!

#### Project and Submission Requirements

Your project must demonstrate the novel creation at the intersection of healthcare and technology that specifically solves an issue. The submissions should demonstrate this problem-solving capabilities in at least a demonstrative capacity.

#### Prizes

TBD. Further announcements will be made soon!

#### Judging Criteria and Winner Selection

1. Idea & Innovation (5 points):

Did the proposal address a real problem within the theme?

Was the idea innovative?

Can this be meaningfully different from other things that exist already?

2. Implementation (5 points):

Does the solution work? Did the team achieve everything they wanted?

How technically challenging/impressive was the implementation?

3. Health Impact & Rigor (5 points):

Who is this for, and would it actually help them? Is there great potential?

4. Design & Usability (5 points):

Did the team put thought into the user experience? How well designed is the interface?

5. Presentation (5 points):

Is the problem clearly defined? Does the demo show the thing actually working? Is the team honest about what's finished versus planned?

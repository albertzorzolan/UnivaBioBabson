**Copyright & Permissions**

Unless otherwise noted, all content on this website, including, without limitation, any and all text, documents, images, graphics, video and sound, is protected copyrighted material and may not be may be reproduced, displayed or distributed, in whole or in part, without prior written permission from the AGS/HiAF.To obtain such written permission, please email a completed [Permission Request Form](https://www.americangeriatrics.org/sites/default/files/inline-files/AGS%20Permissions%20Request%20Form.pdf "American Geriatrics Society AGS Permissions Request Form") to Aimee Cegelka at [acegelka@americangeriatrics.org](mailto: acegelka@americangeriatrics.org).

Permission will be considered on a case-by case basis, and only under the following circumstances:

*   The use of website content is for educational and non-commercial purposes only;
*   None of the website content used is modified in any way;
*   Any graphic images used are not separated from, and are used only in conjunction with, the accompanying text or document; and
*   The website content will not be used in any electronic format.

Any such authorized use of website materials must include an appropriate acknowledgment and citation to the appropriate AGS/HiAF website or product.

For permission to use content from the following AGS/HiAF publications, please contact the person specified.

_Geriatrics Review Syllabus_ or _Geriatrics at Your Fingertips_®  
Email a completed [Permission Request Form](https://www.americangeriatrics.org/sites/default/files/inline-files/AGS%20Permissions%20Request%20Form.pdf "American Geriatrics Society AGS Permissions Request Form")to[Aimee Cegelka](mailto:acegelka@americangeriatrics.org "acegelka@americangeriatrics.org")

_Journal of the American Geriatrics Society_  
For permissions requests, please see guidance [http://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/10.1111/(ISSN)1532-5415/homepage/Permissions.html](http://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/10.1111/(ISSN)1532-5415/homepage/Permissions.html "Journal of the American Geriatrics Society Permissions Info")
